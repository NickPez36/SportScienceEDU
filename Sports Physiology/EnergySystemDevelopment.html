Decoding Human Energy Systems

Bridging the gap between cutting-edge cellular physiology and elite coaching application.

Overview: As an elite performance director, my objective is not merely to understand physiology, but to manipulate it. The traditional model of human energy systems—neatly compartmentalized buckets—is dangerously outdated. Modern physiology reveals a highly fluid, synergistic network of metabolic pathways.

This report deconstructs the three primary energy systems into their modern, highly specific physiological derivatives. By understanding the cellular mechanics, we can precisely target the biggest performance levers and illuminate the blind spots that leave athletes underperforming. We will explore everything from central nervous system drive to mitochondrial capillary networks, culminating in a synthesis of how these systems interact during a maximal 90-second athletic event.
